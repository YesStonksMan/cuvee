Cuvee for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later), macOS 15+, Rosetta 2.

Download the `.pkg` and follow macOS Installer, or drag Cuvee from the `.dmg` into Applications.
No Homebrew, Xcode or terminal is needed to use this edition. Use your own game-launcher accounts.

- Self-contained Wine engine, Metal (DXMT) for DirectX 10/11, OpenGL and Wine Gecko.
- In-app update checks, verified downloads and installation through macOS Installer.
- No user games, bottles, accounts or personal downloads are included. Updates preserve user data.
- Preview: ad-hoc signed, without Developer ID or Apple notarization. macOS may require Open Anyway in Privacy & Security after an initial open attempt. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
- Vulkan, MoltenVK and DirectX 12 are not included. Anti-cheat games may be incompatible.
- Licenses are included in the app; matching sources and SHA-256 checksums accompany the installers.
